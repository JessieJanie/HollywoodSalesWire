name: HSW cold-email campaign format
description: Publisher-approved sequence-length rule for HSW cold outreach
---

All Hollywood Sales Wire cold-email campaigns must use a five-email sequence. Do not draft, configure, or launch a three-email cold campaign.

**Why:** Karilyn explicitly cancelled the three-email approach and confirmed that HSW is doing five-email campaigns only.

**How to apply:** Treat five emails as a hard campaign-format requirement whenever preparing, cloning, reviewing, or launching HSW cold outreach.
